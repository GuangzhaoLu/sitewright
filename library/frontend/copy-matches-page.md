## fe.copy-matches-page

## English

**Cards must tell the truth.** Problem: The home page says "12 lessons, 45 minutes," and the link opens one short article.

**When:** A hub or catalog card summarizes an inner page.

**When not:** The inner page is not written yet. The card should say unfinished. Do not invent a syllabus that does not exist.

**Steps:** Every word on the card must be findable by search on the destination page. If a duration, a lesson count, or a title is not on that page, delete it or change it to what the page actually says.

**Don't:** The inner page has no course plan, and the home page still says 12 weeks.

**Example:** Every number and proper name on a catalog card must be findable on the destination page.

---

## 中文

**卡片不说谎.** 问题：首页写「12 课、45 分钟」，点进去只有一篇短文。

**何时用:** 枢纽或目录卡概括内页。

**何时不用:** 内页还没写，卡上应写「未完成」，不要编一套不存在的课表。

**步骤:** 卡上的词必须能在目标页用搜索找到。时间、课数、篇目若页里没有，就删掉或改成页里真有的说法。

**反例:** 内页没有课程计划，首页仍写 12 周。

**实例:** 目录卡的每一个数字和专名，都要能在目标页找到。
