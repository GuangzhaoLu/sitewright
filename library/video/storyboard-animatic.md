## vid.storyboard-animatic

## English

**Storyboard, then animatic.** Problem: Someone starts cutting a three-minute piece.

**Steps:** One line per shot: who, what they see, how long it holds, the caption. Show the conductor an 8fps draft first, then the fine cut. Appetite: one piece this round, at most 30 seconds.

---

## 中文

**分镜 → 动态草稿.** 问题：直接剪 3 分钟。

**步骤:** 每镜一行：谁、看什么、停多久、字幕。先 8fps 草稿给指挥看，再精剪。胃口：这一轮一条 ≤30 秒。
