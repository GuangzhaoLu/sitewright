## vid.loop-seam

## English

**Loop seam.** Problem: The loop jumps at the join, and it looks broken.

**When:** A silent loop.

**When not:** An explanation video that does not loop.

**Steps:**

1. The first frame and the last frame share a composition, or you cut on a held frame.
2. Do not cut captions at the join. Keep words out of the picture on a loop when you can.
3. Watch the join three times. If it jumps, switch to a still.

**Don't:** An ink drop that snaps back to the start at the join.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet.

## 中文

**循环接缝.** 问题：循环在接头跳一下，看起来像坏了。

**何时用:** 无声循环。

**何时不用:** 不循环的讲解片。

**步骤:**

1. 第一帧和最后一帧构图相同，或在静止格切。
2. 不要在接头切字幕。循环片的字尽量不要烧进画面。
3. 连看三次接头。跳就改静帧。

**反例:** 墨滴在接头突然回到原点。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。
