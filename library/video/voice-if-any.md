## vid.voice-if-any

## English

**Voice, if any.** Problem: Noise floor and music cover the sentence.

**When:** The brief explicitly wants a spoken telling.

**When not:** A silent loop. Do not lay music under it by default.

**Steps:**

1. The voice is dry and close, with no background music. Music competes with reading on the page.
2. Sentences are short, and they match the full text beside the player.
3. Sound is opened by the user. Autoplay stays silent.
4. If a synthetic voice reads a quotation that must be exact, a person checks the words before it ships.

**Don't:** Autoplay of a voiceover with music.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Autoplay with sound is blocked by the browser. See `res.video-methods-search`.

## 中文

**若有人声.** 问题：底噪和配乐盖过句子。

**何时用:** 简报明确要人声讲述。

**何时不用:** 无声循环。默认不要铺底乐。

**步骤:**

1. 人声干、近、无背景音乐。音乐会和页面上的阅读抢注意力。
2. 句子短，和旁侧全文一致。
3. 有声必须由用户打开。自动播放保持无声。
4. 合成人声若念必须准确的引文，要人工对过字再上。

**反例:** 自动播放带配乐的旁白。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。有声自动播放会被浏览器拦住，见 `res.video-methods-search`。
