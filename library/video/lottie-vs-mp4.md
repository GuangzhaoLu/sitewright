## vid.lottie-vs-mp4

## English

**Lottie or MP4.**

| Need | Choose |
|---|---|
| Icons, progress, a small state | CSS or Lottie |
| Texture, live footage, a continuous material | MP4 or WebM |
| An explanation that can pause | Prefer stepped HTML. Do not make a video |

Do not stuff a whole story into Lottie. Give an MP4 to `<video>`. Do not use it as a CSS background that cannot carry captions.

---

## 中文

**矢量动效对栅格片.**

| 需求 | 选 |
|---|---|
| 图标、进度、小状态 | CSS 或 Lottie |
| 纹理、实拍、连续材质 | MP4/WebM |
| 可暂停的讲解 | 宁可用分步 HTML，不上片 |

Lottie 不要塞整段故事。MP4 给 `<video>`，不要当 CSS background 又无法字幕。
