## vid.micro-three

## English

**Three short beats.** Problem: You want to make one misunderstanding clear, and it turns into an intro film.

**When:** About twenty seconds can walk "the common view → where it fails → where to look instead."

**When not:** The evidence needs a long passage. Use a round or a body page. Do not make a video.

**Steps:**

1. Three shots. One sentence each.
2. The second shot lets the misunderstanding fail. Do not turn the misunderstanding into a quotable line.
3. The third shot points at a playable round or a body page. Do not recite the evidence inside the video.

**Don't:** Ninety seconds, with music under each of the three parts.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. If the misunderstanding has to be tapped wrong by the player, use `game.myth-trap`. Do not replace that with a video.

---

## 中文

**微三段.** 问题：想讲清一个误解，却写成介绍片。

**何时用:** 约 20 秒内能走完「常见看法 → 何处不对 → 改看哪里」。

**何时不用:** 证据需要长段。那用一局或正文页，不上片。

**步骤:**

1. 三镜，每镜一句。
2. 第二镜让误解失败，不把误解讲成金句。
3. 第三镜指向可玩的局或正文页，不在片里把证据念完。

**反例:** 九十秒、三段各有配乐。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。误解若要玩家亲手点错，用 `game.myth-trap`，不要用片代替。
