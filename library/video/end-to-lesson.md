## vid.end-to-lesson

## English

**End on the next page.** Problem: The video ends on black, or it chains to another recommended video.

**When:** The video is a step before the next page.

**When not:** A pure atmosphere loop, with no "finished watching."

**Steps:**

1. The end holds on the poster or the last frame, with a link beside it to a chapter that exists now.
2. Do not autoplay the next one. Do not embed a third-party recommendation.
3. The link target has to open (see `web.exit-to-lesson`). It is not "being written."

**Don't:** A subscribe card at the end, or the next video starting by itself.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet.

---

## 中文

**看完去下一页.** 问题：片结束在黑场，或连到别的推荐片。

**何时用:** 片承担进下一页之前的一步。

**何时不用:** 纯氛围循环，没有「看完」。

**步骤:**

1. 结束停在海报或最后一格，旁边是到现网章节的链接。
2. 不自动连播，不嵌第三方推荐。
3. 链接目标要打得开（见 `web.exit-to-lesson`），不是「撰写中」。

**反例:** 片尾订阅，或下一支自动播放。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。
