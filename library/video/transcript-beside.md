## vid.transcript-beside

## English

**Transcript beside the player.** Problem: Captions flash past. They cannot be copied, and they cannot be searched.

**When:** Any public video that carries information.

**When not:** An internal screen recording.

**Steps:**

1. The page has the full narration or the storyboard sentences, visible by default, not only behind a captions button.
2. When the video is an atmosphere loop, the text beside it is still that shot's one sentence. It does not tell a second story.
3. The full text and the captions match. A changed sentence is changed in both places.

**Don't:** Words exist only burned into the picture, with no copyable text on the page.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Besides `vid.captions-poster`, the body has a copy too.

## 中文

**旁侧全文.** 问题：字幕一闪而过，无法复制，也无法检索。

**何时用:** 任何上线、带信息的片。

**何时不用:** 内部录屏。

**步骤:**

1. 页上有完整旁白或分镜句子，默认可见，不只藏在字幕按钮里。
2. 片是氛围循环时，旁侧仍是那一镜的一句，不另讲一套。
3. 全文与字幕一致。改一句要两处一起改。

**反例:** 字只烧在画面里，页面上没有可复制文本。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。在 `vid.captions-poster` 之外，正文里再有一份。
