## vid.step-stills

## English

**Stepped stills.** Problem: A process was made into a video that cannot pause.

**When:** The process is three to five stable states (a layer opens, a card falls in, a line splits).

**When not:** There is no frame between states where you can hold.

**Steps:**

1. Each state is one still or one DOM state, with a "next" button.
2. One sentence per step. The player can go back.
3. This is not a video file. Do not autoplay through it.
4. If the brief bans a how-to you can practice, the steps show a judgment, not a drill.

**Don't:** Ten-plus seconds of autoplay, with the middle states unreadable.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. When the process needs to hold, prefer this over `vid.silent-loop`.

## 中文

**分步静帧.** 问题：一个过程被做成不能暂停的片。

**何时用:** 过程是 3–5 个稳定状态（层打开、卡落入、线分开）。

**何时不用:** 状态之间没有可停留的一格。

**步骤:**

1. 每状态一张静帧或一个 DOM 状态，按钮「下一步」。
2. 每步一句。玩家可以回退。
3. 这不是视频文件，不要自动播完。
4. 简报若禁止可跟练的操作步骤，分步只展示判断，不展示练法。

**反例:** 自动播放十几秒，中间态看不清。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。过程需要停时，优先于 `vid.silent-loop`。
