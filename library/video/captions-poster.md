## vid.captions-poster

## English

**Captions and poster.** Problem: No captions means no video. The poster is the still that best stands for the piece. Its filename goes in the HTML `poster=`.

Burn Chinese captions into the picture only when you cannot ship WebVTT. A track is better, because it can be turned off.

---

## 中文

**字幕与海报.** 问题：无字幕等于无视频。海报是静帧里最能代表的一格，文件名写进 HTML `poster=`。

中文字幕烧进画面仅当没有能力上 WebVTT；能上轨更好（可关）。
