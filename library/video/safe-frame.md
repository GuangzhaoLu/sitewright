## vid.safe-frame

## English

**Safe frame.** Problem: Captions sit on the edge, and a narrow screen or the system controls cut the words off.

**When:** Words must be burned into the picture. Prefer not to burn them.

**When not:** The words are in HTML.

**Steps:**

1. Put words inside about the central eighty percent. Do not sit them on the bottom edge.
2. Preview once at 16:9, and once in a narrow viewport.
3. If it can become text beside the picture, change it.

**Don't:** A full-width caption along the bottom, covered by phone controls.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Prefer `vid.transcript-beside`.

---

## 中文

**安全框.** 问题：字幕贴边，窄屏和系统控件把字切掉。

**何时用:** 字必须烧进画面时。尽量不要烧。

**何时不用:** 字在 HTML 里。

**步骤:**

1. 字放在画面中央约八成的框内，不贴底边。
2. 用 16:9 预览一次，用窄视口再预览一次。
3. 能改成旁侧文字就改掉。

**反例:** 底部通栏字幕，在手机上被控件挡住。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。优先 `vid.transcript-beside`。
