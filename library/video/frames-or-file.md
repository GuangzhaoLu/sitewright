## vid.frames-or-file

## English

**Frames or one file.** Problem: A few stable states were made into one video that cannot stop.

**When:** After you have decided it must move, and before you pick a container.

**When not:** You have already decided it does not move.

**Steps:**

1. Fewer than about eight states, and each state must be able to hold: use stepped stills or CSS. Do not make a video file.
2. Texture, live footage, or a continuous material: then use MP4 or WebM.
3. Icons and progress: CSS or Lottie (see `vid.lottie-vs-mp4`).
4. After you choose, write the reason as one line in the brief.

**Don't:** Exporting several megabytes of video for four states.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet.

---

## 中文

**逐帧还是一个片文件.** 问题：几个稳定状态被做成一条不能停的片。

**何时用:** 决定要动之后、选容器之前。

**何时不用:** 已经决定不要动。

**步骤:**

1. 状态少于约 8 格、每格要停：用分步静帧或 CSS，不上片。
2. 纹理、实拍、连续材质：才用 MP4/WebM。
3. 图标和进度：CSS 或 Lottie（见 `vid.lottie-vs-mp4`）。
4. 选完把理由写进简报一行。

**反例:** 为四格状态导出数兆视频。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。
