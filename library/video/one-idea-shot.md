## vid.one-idea-shot

## English

**One idea per shot.** Problem: One shot packs a claim, the evidence, and a lyric mood at once.

**When:** You have already decided to make a video.

**When not:** It has not passed `vid.when-not`.

**Steps:**

1. Each shot in the board is one sentence: what the viewer should know when this shot ends.
2. If you cannot write that sentence, delete the shot.
3. The whole piece still stays within about 30 seconds (see `vid.storyboard-animatic`).

**Don't:** One shot that pans from a wide view to a close-up and stacks three lines of captions.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet.

---

## 中文

**一镜一意.** 问题：一个镜头里同时塞主张、证据和抒情。

**何时用:** 已经决定要做片。

**何时不用:** 还没通过 `vid.when-not`。

**步骤:**

1. 分镜每镜只写一个句子：这一镜结束后观众应知道什么。
2. 写不出那句，就删这一镜。
3. 整条仍守约 30 秒以内（见 `vid.storyboard-animatic`）。

**反例:** 一镜从全景摇到特写，再叠三行字幕。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。
