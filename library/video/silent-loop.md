## vid.silent-loop

## English

**Silent loop.** Problem: Autoplay with sound is blocked by the system on a phone, or it startles someone.

**Steps:** `muted` + `playsinline` + `loop`. A poster frame is **required**. Equivalent information still exists in text beside it. Duration 3–8 seconds. Do not put the thing to learn only in the audio track.

**Don't:** The thing to learn appears only in the spoken line.

---

## 中文

**无声循环.** 问题：移动端自动播放带声被系统拦，或吓到人。

**步骤:** `muted` + `playsinline` + `loop`；**必须**有海报帧；旁边仍有文字等价信息。时长 3–8 秒。不要依赖声轨讲知识点。

**反例:** 知识点只出现在台词里。
