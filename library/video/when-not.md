## vid.when-not

## English

**Decide against video first.** Problem: A stretch of autoplay is added so the page feels complete.

**When:** A spatial relation has to unfold in time (for example, appearing side by side), or you need a person speaking within about 20 seconds.

**When not:** One image plus one sentence can finish it, or it is a decorative background film.

**Steps:** Write one sentence: "if this video is missing, what will the player misunderstand?" If you cannot write it, cut the video.

---

## 中文

**先决定不要视频.** 问题：为了「完整」上一段自动播放。

**何时用:** 空间关系必须在时间里展开（例如并排出现），或需要 20 秒内的真人讲述。

**何时不用:** 一张图 + 一句能讲完；装饰性背景大片。

**步骤:** 写一句「若无此片，玩家会误解什么」。写不出来就砍。
