## res.analog-first

## English

**Tabletop before screen.** Problem: If it can be played through on a table with cards and trays, then decide the DOM. Search: sorting tray classroom, concept cards, wrong popular answer.

If the table needs a referee who keeps changing the rules, the spec is not frozen.
