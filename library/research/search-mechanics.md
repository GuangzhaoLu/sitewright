## res.search-mechanics

## English

**Search by mechanic, not by topic.**

Good words: classification game, odd one out, sequence order, highlight the claim, claim and evidence, continuum scale, concept mapping, image hotspot, confidence calibration, spot the revision, two-axis matrix, repair one word, proportion split, filter layers, short consequence fork, quote back to source, margin labels, set collection, you draw it, branching timeline, source criticism activity.

Poor words: the subject name plus "game," or "cool educational game."

Source order:

1. The Sitewright library
2. News interactives already online (NYT / Guardian). Read the mechanic, not the news
3. BoardGameGeek mechanic tags, and classroom activities (sorting cards, concept maps)
4. itch.io tags (mind the license; learn the mechanic only)
5. GDC Vault talks at the level of a public description (if login is required, note the title for later; do not pretend you watched it)

## 中文

**按机制搜，不按题材搜.**

好词：classification game、odd one out、sequence order、highlight the claim、claim and evidence、continuum scale、concept mapping、image hotspot、confidence calibration、spot the revision、two-axis matrix、repair one word、proportion split、filter layers、short consequence fork、quote back to source、margin labels、set collection、you draw it、branching timeline、source criticism activity。

差词：只搜题材名加「游戏」，或「cool educational game」。

来源优先级：

1. Sitewright 库
2. 已上线的新闻互动（NYT / Guardian 互动，看机制不看新闻）
3. BoardGameGeek 机制标签、课堂活动设计（分类卡、概念图）
4. itch.io 标签（注意授权，只学机制）
5. GDC Vault 通告级演讲（要登录则记下题目以后看，不要假称已看完）
