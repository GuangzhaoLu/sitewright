## res.competitor-playthrough

## English

**Play the loop only.** Problem: Open two sites of the same kind. For each, note what the entrance feels like in three seconds, how long one round takes, what happens on a wrong answer, and where the exit goes. Do not copy their words or pictures. Turn "what happens when it is wrong" into our fail spec.

---

## 中文

**只玩循环.** 问题：打开 2 个同类站，各记：入口 3 秒感知、一局多久、错了发生什么、出口去哪。不抄文案、不抄图。把「错了发生什么」写成我们的失败规格。
