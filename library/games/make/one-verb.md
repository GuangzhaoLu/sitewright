## game.one-verb

## English

**One verb.** Problem: The round teaches three gestures before it teaches the idea.

**When:** You are building any kind.
**When not:** Never as an excuse to add a second gesture. A richer kind is still one verb per screen.

**Steps:**

1. The first line names the verb and nothing else: tap then tap, choose, match, reveal.
2. Repeat that verb for every item. Do not add a second gesture to make it deeper.
3. If a second verb is actually required, it is the next round.

**Don't:** Drag, type, and shake inside the same few seconds.

**Example:** Sort trays is tap the card, then tap the tray, on every card.

## 中文

**一个动作.** 问题：这一局先教了三种手势，还没教到观念。

**何时用:** 在做任何一种小游戏时。
**何时不用:** 不能拿这张卡当借口再加第二个手势。再丰富的种类，同一屏也只有一个动作。

**步骤:**

1. 第一行只写出动作：点再点、选择、配对、揭开。
2. 每一项都重复这个动作。不要为了「更深」再加一种手势。
3. 若确实需要第二个动作，那是下一局。

**反例:** 几秒之内又拖、又打字、又晃。

**实例:** 分层托盘是每张卡都先点卡片，再点托盘。
