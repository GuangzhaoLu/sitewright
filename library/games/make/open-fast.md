## game.open-fast

## English

**Obvious in one line.** Problem: The player looks at the board and does not know what to press.

**When:** The first screen of a round.
**When not:** A separate rules page. The round itself still has to open in one line.

**Steps:**

1. The first sentence is the verb and the win.
2. The first tappable thing is obvious. No tutorial modal.
3. If a sample is needed, the first item is already half done, and the next tap is the one that is highlighted.

**Don't:** A paragraph of instructions above a board that does not respond.

**Example:** "Tap the card, then the tray it belongs in. The trays are labeled."

## 中文

**一眼知道按什么.** 问题：玩家看着棋盘，不知道按哪里。

**何时用:** 一局的第一屏。
**何时不用:** 单独的规则页。规则页不能代替这一局自己的第一句。

**步骤:**

1. 第一句就是动作和怎样算完成。
2. 第一个能点的东西很明显。不要先弹教程。
3. 若需要示范，第一项已经做了一半，下一步高亮的就是该点的地方。

**反例:** 上面一大段说明，下面的棋盘还不能动。

**实例:** 「点卡片，再点它该去的托盘。托盘上写着标准。」
