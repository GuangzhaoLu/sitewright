## game.light-juice

## English

**A small confirmation.** Problem: The round is correct and lifeless, or it is a carnival.

**When:** You want the tap to feel confirmed.
**When not:** The motion would be the only carrier of the meaning. If motion is reduced, the words still have to work.

**Steps:**

1. A right placement gets a short move: the card settles, the pair stays. Under about 200ms.
2. A wrong tap gets a small return, plus the sentence from `game.answer-now`.
3. If reduced motion is requested, keep the words and drop the travel.

**Don't:** Particles, a shaking screen, neon, or a sound that plays by itself.

**Example:** The tray accepts the card with a short drop. The sentence is what teaches.

## 中文

**轻一点的确认.** 问题：这一局是对的，但没有手感；或者手感做成了庙会。

**何时用:** 希望这一下点按被确认。
**何时不用:** 动作成了唯一传递意思的东西。减少动效之后，文字仍要成立。

**步骤:**

1. 放对时有一个短动作：卡片落下，一对留着。大约 200 毫秒以内。
2. 点错时轻轻退回，再加上 `game.answer-now` 的那一句。
3. 若读者要求减少动效，留下文字，去掉位移。

**反例:** 粒子、整屏震动、霓虹，或自己响起来的声音。

**实例:** 托盘用一个短落下收下卡片。教会人的是那句话。
