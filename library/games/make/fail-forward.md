## game.fail-forward

## English

**A miss still teaches.** Problem: A wrong tap ends the round or shames the player.

**When:** The round has a wrong answer.
**When not:** The brief says this item is a trap that must not `mark` (`game.myth-trap`). The trap still explains itself. It does not insult.

**Steps:**

1. A miss does not wipe other items that were already right.
2. The miss teaches one thing. The same item can be tried again, or it returns once after the next item.
3. No timer, no life counter, no "you failed."

**Don't:** A red screen whose only action is restart.

**Example:** A mismatched pair turns back, and one line says these two do not name the same thing.

---

## 中文

**点错还在教.** 问题：点错就结束这一局，或让玩家难堪。

**何时用:** 这一局有错误答案。
**何时不用:** 简报写明这一项是不得 `mark` 的陷阱（`game.myth-trap`）。陷阱仍要说明自己，但不羞辱。

**步骤:**

1. 一次点错不清掉已经做对的项。
2. 点错教会一件事。同一项可以再试，或在下一项之后再出现一次。
3. 没有计时，没有生命条，没有「你失败了」。

**反例:** 一块红屏，唯一的动作是重来。

**实例:** 一对没配上就翻回去，一行字说这两张不是同一件事。
