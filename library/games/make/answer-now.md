## game.answer-now

## English

**Answer in the same view.** Problem: The player taps and nothing they can read changes.

**When:** Every tap that can be right or wrong.
**When not:** A tap that only moves focus.

**Steps:**

1. In the same view, say what happened, in words. Color may sit beside the words.
2. A hit says why it counts. A miss says the miss in one sentence, not only a buzz.
3. The board updates so the words and the picture agree: the card settles, the pair stays, the cover clears.

**Don't:** A score pop with no sentence. A delay so an animation can finish before the words.

**Example:** A wrong tray sends the card back, and one line says which tray it belongs in.

---

## 中文

**当下就有回应.** 问题：点了之后，读得见的东西没有变化。

**何时用:** 每一次可能对也可能错的点按。
**何时不用:** 这一下只是把焦点移走。

**步骤:**

1. 就在这一屏里，用文字说发生了什么。颜色可以陪着文字。
2. 点对要说为什么算数。点错要用一句话说差在哪，不能只有一声提示。
3. 棋盘跟着变，文字和图一致：卡片落下、一对留着、遮盖揭开。

**反例:** 只有分数弹出，没有句子。为了等动画放完而推迟文字。

**实例:** 托盘放错，卡片回去，一行字写明它该去哪一盘。
