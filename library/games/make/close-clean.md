## game.close-clean

## English

**A clean ending.** Problem: The round is over and the player is still on a board with nowhere to go.

**When:** The win condition has been met.
**When not:** The player is still in the middle of an item.

**Steps:**

1. Say it is done in one sentence that restates the idea. Not "You win!"
2. Link to the next real page named in the brief. Replay is a secondary text link.
3. If progress exists, `mark` once. Replay does not clear it.

**Don't:** A loot box, a streak, or a share dialog as the ending.

**Example:** "These three were records, not stories." Then a link into the article.

---

## 中文

**做完就收.** 问题：这一局结束了，人还停在棋盘上，没有去处。

**何时用:** 通关条件已经达到。
**何时不用:** 还在做某一项的中途。

**步骤:**

1. 用一句话收束，这句话重述这一局的观念。不要只写「你赢了」。
2. 链到简报指定的下一张真页面。再来一次是次要的文字链。
3. 若有进度，`mark` 一次。再玩不清掉它。

**反例:** 用抽奖、连胜或分享框当结尾。

**实例:** 「这三张是记录，不是故事。」然后链进正文。
