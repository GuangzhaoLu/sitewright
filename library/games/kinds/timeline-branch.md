## game.timeline-branch

## English

**Branching timeline.** Problem: Chronological order is read as a causal upgrade.

**When:** You need to see two lines in the same years at once.

**When not:** You only need to remember one year.

**Steps:** Years on the horizontal axis, traditions on the vertical. An event card can stick to "side by side," not to "the next boss." No experience bar.

**Don't:** A map that says you must clear this region before the next one unlocks.

---

## 中文

**分叉时间线.** 问题：年代顺序被理解成因果升级。

**何时用:** 要同时看到两条线在同一年代。

**何时不用:** 只需记一个年份。

**步骤:** 横轴年代，纵轴传统。事件卡只能贴在「并排」而不是「下一个 Boss」。禁止经验条。

**反例:** 地图做成「通关这一区才解锁下一区」。
