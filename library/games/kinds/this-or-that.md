## game.this-or-that

## English

**This or that.** Problem: Two options, and the reader will not work through a comparison table.

**When:** A short run of binary judgments, and each prompt states the criterion.
**When not:** There are more than two real choices, or the pair needs a long essay before any tap.

**Steps:**

1. A and B are two large buttons. The question sits above them.
2. A tap gets one line: why this side, or why not.
3. Three to seven pairs. The same verb every time.
4. `mark` when the answers named in the spec are all chosen. No streak multiplier.

**Don't:** A swipe that is the only way to answer. Tap must work.

**Example:** "Which of these two belongs with the claim," repeated down one section.

## 中文

**二选一.** 问题：只有两个选项，读者不会去读对照表。

**何时用:** 一串二选一的判断，每一题的标准写在题干上。
**何时不用:** 实际不止两个选择，或没读完一段长文就无法点。

**步骤:**

1. A 和 B 是两个大按钮。问题写在上面。
2. 点下去给一行：为什么是这边，或为什么不是。
3. 三到七对。每次都是同一个动作。
4. 规格里的答案都选中才 `mark`。不要连击加分。

**反例:** 只能靠滑动来答。点按必须能完成。

**实例:** 「这两个里哪一个配得上这句主张」，在同一节里重复几次。
