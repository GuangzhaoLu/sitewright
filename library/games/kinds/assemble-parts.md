## game.assemble-parts

## English

**Assemble the parts.** Problem: The reader can name the parts and still cannot see how they sit together.

**When:** One silhouette and three to six pieces. Each piece has one correct slot.
**When not:** The lesson is only a definition, or there are more than six pieces.

**Steps:**

1. The silhouette shows empty slots. Pieces are buttons beside it, as simple shapes or thumbnails, not paragraphs.
2. Tap a piece, then tap a slot. A fit locks with one sentence. A miss says what is wrong with the position, then the piece returns.
3. Tap must be enough. Drag may assist.
4. `mark` when every slot is filled as the spec says.

**Don't:** A free-floating jigsaw of dozens of pieces, or a piece that only fits by a few pixels.

**Example:** A tool, a room, or a simple machine. Slot ids and piece ids live in JSON. The page section is the board.

## 中文

**把零件拼上.** 问题：读者叫得出零件的名字，却看不出它们怎样拼在一起。

**何时用:** 一个轮廓，三到六块零件，每块只有一个正确的槽。
**何时不用:** 只要背定义，或零件超过六块。

**步骤:**

1. 轮廓上是空槽。零件是旁边的按钮，用简单形状或缩略图，不用一整段文字。
2. 先点零件，再点槽。合上就锁住，并给一句说明。不合就说位置错在哪，零件回去。
3. 点按必须能完成。拖拽只是辅助。
4. 每个槽都按规格填上才 `mark`。

**反例:** 几十块自由漂浮的拼图，或只有差几个像素才算合上。

**实例:** 一件工具、一间房间或一台简单的机器。槽和零件的 id 在 JSON 里。这一节就是棋盘。
