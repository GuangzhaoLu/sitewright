## game.repair-sentence

## English

**Repair one word.** Problem: The sentence sounds smooth, and the error is one word.

**When:** Replacing one word in a sentence turns the misunderstanding into the accurate wording the brief wants. At most three replacement candidates.

**When not:** The whole paragraph has to be rewritten. If the whole sentence is a named popular trap, use `myth-trap`.

**Steps:**

1. The wrong word is a button. After it is tapped, three replacements appear. Tap the right one.
2. Tapping another word: say that word is not the problem in this sentence.
3. After the swap, read the whole sentence again so the player sees the new sentence.
4. `mark` only when the swap is right.

**Don't:** Four words are all "also fine," and there is no single accurate replacement.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Shorter than `term-match`. Only one sentence moves.

## 中文

**改一句.** 问题：整句听着顺，错在一个词。

**何时用:** 一句里换掉一个词，误解就变成简报要的准确说法。候选替换 ≤3。

**何时不用:** 要重写整段。整句是点名的流行陷阱——用 `myth-trap`。

**步骤:**

1. 错词是按钮。点中后出现 3 个替换，点对的那个。
2. 点到别的词：说明它不是这句的问题。
3. 换对之后整句重读一遍，让玩家看见新句。
4. 换对才 `mark`。

**反例:** 四个词都「也可以」，没有唯一准确替换。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。比 `term-match` 更短，只动一句。
