## game.hotspot

## English

**Image hotspots.** Problem: A spatial relation is written out in a long caption, and the picture is decoration.

**When:** One still has at most six regions. Each hit teaches one sentence, and the hit area can be about 44px.

**When not:** The regions are too small or too dense, or the picture does not exist yet (make the diagram first, then the hotspots).

**Steps:**

1. A hotspot is a transparent button over the image, with a visible focus.
2. A hit: one line of narration. Do not navigate the whole page away. A tap on empty space: no penalty.
3. On a narrow screen, switch to a button list under the image, using the same ids, so hit areas do not stack.
4. The brief says whether every region must be visited before `mark`.

**Don't:** Hunting a tiny symbol on a distant atmosphere shot.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. A museum-label hotspot. Do not draw anything the brief bans onto the picture.

## 中文

**图上热点.** 问题：空间关系写在长说明里，图只是装饰。

**何时用:** 一张静帧上有 ≤6 个区域，点中各教一句，且热区能做到约 44px。

**何时不用:** 区域太小、太密，或图还没有（先做图解再做热点）。

**步骤:**

1. 热点是透明按钮叠在图上，有可见焦点。
2. 点对：旁白一句，不弹走整个页面。点空处：无惩罚。
3. 窄屏改成图下按钮列表，点同一批 id，避免热区叠死。
4. 简报写明要不要全部点过才 `mark`。

**反例:** 在氛围远景上找极小的符号。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。博物馆展板热点。图上不画简报禁止的东西。
