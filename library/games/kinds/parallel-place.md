## game.parallel-place

## English

**Place side by side.** Problem: The reader draws parallel traditions as a ladder of bosses.

**When:** You need to correct "one line of evolution."

**When not:** It really is the history of one institution, and the brief allows a single line.

**Steps:**

1. Name slots as a side branch, an event seat, or a side path. Do not call them "the next stage."
2. Tap a card, then tap a slot. Some cards are forbidden on the trunk. The explanation says why.
3. If "upgrade to the next stage" appears, it only fails. It does not `mark`.
4. The win: every card is in the slot the spec names, and nothing forbidden sits on the trunk.

**Don't:** An experience bar, an evolution animation, or passing even when the forbidden object was placed "correctly."

**Example:** The kind the brief says cannot enter the trunk does not become an upgrade when it is placed correctly.

## 中文

**并排放置.** 问题：读者把并列传统画成升级打怪。

**何时用:** 要纠正「一条进化线」。

**何时不用:** 真的是单一制度史且简报允许单线。

**步骤:**

1. 槽位命名为并排枝 / 事件席 / 旁支，不要叫「下一阶段」。
2. 点卡再点槽。某类卡禁止进主干，讲解要说清为什么。
3. 「升级成下一阶段」若出现，只失败、不 `mark`。
4. 通关条件：每张卡在规格写明的槽，主干上没有禁物。

**反例:** 经验条、进化动画、把禁物放对了也过关。

**实例:** 简报点名不能进主干的那一类，放对了也不算升级。
