## game.proportion-split

## English

**Split a proportion.** Problem: A composition is remembered as "a lot" or "a little."

**When:** There is a share you can check, at most four parts, and they add up to the whole.

**When not:** There is no number. Do not force `you-draw-it` onto it either.

**Steps:**

1. Offer a few preset splits as buttons, or a step of "add one piece / remove one piece." No precise dragging.
2. Reveal the real proportion, and say which piece was off.
3. The tolerance is in the brief (for example, one step off still passes). Do not require pixel alignment.
4. `mark` only inside the tolerance.

**Don't:** Ask the player to type a percentage to the ones place.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. A button version of estimating a proportion, not drawing a curve.

## 中文

**比例分割.** 问题：构成被记成「很多」或「很少」。

**何时用:** 有可核对的份额，份数 ≤4，加总为整。

**何时不用:** 没有数。那也不要硬套 `you-draw-it`。

**步骤:**

1. 提供几套预设分割（按钮），或「加一块 / 减一块」步进。不要精密拖拽。
2. 揭晓真实比例，并说差在哪一块。
3. 容差写在简报（例如差一档仍过）。不要求像素对齐。
4. 在容差内才 `mark`。

**反例:** 让玩家输入百分数到个位。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。比例估计的按钮版，不是画曲线。
