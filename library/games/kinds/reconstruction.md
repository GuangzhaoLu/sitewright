## game.reconstruction

## English

**Rebuild the structure.** Problem: The reader thinks the text is one comforting paragraph.

**When:** You are teaching passage structure or the levels of a table of contents.

**When not:** The source is long and has no clear blocks.

**Steps:** Keep the blocks few (at most seven). A wrong placement says something like "this block is a note, not the main text."

---

## 中文

**拼回结构.** 问题：读者以为文本是一段鸡汤。

**何时用:** 要教「篇章结构 / 目录层次」。

**何时不用:** 原文很长且没有清晰块。

**步骤:** 块要少（≤7）。错位给出「这块是注释，不是正文」一类话。
