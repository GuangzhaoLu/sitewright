## game.compare-two

## English

**Compare two.** Problem: Two objects that are easy to mix up.

**When:** Exactly one pair, and one distinction you can act on.

**When not:** More than three pairs — switch to `term-match`.

**Steps:** Under the same question, tap A or B. The criterion is in the prompt. It is not revealed after the win.

---

## 中文

**两相对照.** 问题：两个易混对象。

**何时用:** 恰好一对，且有一条可操作的区分标准。

**何时不用:** 超过三对——改 `term-match`。

**步骤:** 同一问题下点 A 或 B。标准写在题干，不藏在通关后。
