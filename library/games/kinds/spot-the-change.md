## game.spot-the-change

## English

**Spot the changed claim.** Problem: After a rewrite, the reader thinks it is still the same sentence.

**When:** Two passages side by side, at most five changes, and the changes are of meaning, not punctuation.

**When not:** A typo hunt; or the whole sentence is a popular line named in the brief — use `myth-trap`.

**Steps:**

1. Two columns. The tappable targets are the changed phrase buttons, not arbitrary characters.
2. A hit: say how the claim before and the claim after differ. A tap on an unchanged spot: a light note, "these two match here."
3. `mark` only when every changed claim has been found.
4. On a narrow screen, stack them: the original, then the rewrite.

**Don't:** Twenty differences of punctuation and wording, which becomes an eyesight test.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Unlike `highlight-span`, there must be a rewritten version.

## 中文

**两文找改口.** 问题：改写之后读者以为还是同一句。

**何时用:** 左右两段，改动 ≤5 处，改的是含义不是标点。

**何时不用:** 错别字游戏；或整句就是简报点名的流行话——用 `myth-trap`。

**步骤:**

1. 两栏对照。可点的是被改过的那几个短语按钮，不是任意字符。
2. 点中：说明改前主张与改后主张差在哪。点到未改处：轻提示「这处两文一样」。
3. 全部改口点中才 `mark`。
4. 窄屏改为上下叠，先原文后改写。

**反例:** 二十处标点和用词差异，变成视力测试。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。与 `highlight-span` 不同：这里必有一个被改过的版本。
