## game.source-grade

## English

**Grade the source.** Problem: An inscription, an official history, a novel, and a film are treated as the same grade of citation.

**When:** Teaching a ladder of citation.

**When not:** When this repeats `sort-trays`, pick one of the two rounds.

**Steps:** At most four grades. Drag may assist. Tap must be enough to pass. A miss points out that "handsome is not the same as usable as a note."

---

## 中文

**给材料评级.** 问题：碑刻、正史、小说、影视被看成同一级引用。

**何时用:** 教学「引用阶梯」。

**何时不用:** 与 `sort-trays` 重复时，两局选一。

**步骤:** 4 级以内。拖拽可做辅，点选必须能过关。错：指出「好看≠可作注」。
