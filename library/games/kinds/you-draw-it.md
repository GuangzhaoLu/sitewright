## game.you-draw-it

## English

**You draw it.** Problem: The reader is too sure about a trend. Source: NYT *You Draw It*.

**When:** There is a curve or a proportion you can check.

**When not:** There is no data, only an attitude.

**Steps:** Let the player draw or estimate first, then reveal the true value, then one sentence of explanation. On a phone, tap a few control points. Do not require a precise pen stroke. A small site may use three choices instead of a real drawing.

**Don't:** Passing only when the drawing matches to the pixel.

---

## 中文

**你来画.** 问题：读者对趋势过度自信。来源：NYT *You Draw It*。

**何时用:** 有一条可核对的曲线或比例。

**何时不用:** 没有数据，只有态度。

**步骤:** 先让玩家画/估 → 揭晓真值 → 一句解释。移动端用点几个控制点，不要精密笔绘。网页小站可用三档选择代替真的画。

**反例:** 要求像素级画对才过关。
