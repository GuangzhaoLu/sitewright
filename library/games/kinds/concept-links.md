## game.concept-links

## English

**Labeled concept links.** Problem: A relation is remembered as a string of nouns. You cannot see what one thing is to another.

**When:** At most eight nodes, and the relations have type names (comes from, comments on, at the same time, opposes).

**When not:** The relation is only "related." If it would be drawn as one line of evolution, use `parallel-place`.

**Steps:**

1. Tap the start, tap the end, then tap a relation label. Labels are buttons, not free text.
2. A wrong relation: say what the relation actually is, or that they do not connect.
3. Do not allow a chain "from lower to higher."
4. `mark` only when every edge in the spec is connected correctly.

**Don't:** A spider web where linking every node to every node still counts as correct.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. A classroom concept map. Learn the linking mechanic only.

## 中文

**概念连线.** 问题：关系被记成一串名词，看不出谁对谁是什么。

**何时用:** 节点 ≤8，关系有类型名（出自、注释、同时、反对）。

**何时不用:** 关系只是「相关」。要画成一条进化线时改 `parallel-place`。

**步骤:**

1. 点起点，点终点，再点关系标签。标签是按钮，不是自由输入。
2. 错误关系：指出这两点实际是哪一种关系，或根本不连。
3. 禁止连成「从低级到高级」。
4. 规格里的边全部连对才 `mark`。

**反例:** 蜘蛛网，每点都连每点也算对。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。概念图课堂；只学连线机制。
