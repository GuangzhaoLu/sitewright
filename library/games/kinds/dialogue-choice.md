## game.dialogue-choice

## English

**Dialogue choice.** Problem: The idea lives only in an essay. The player never feels present.

**When:** There is one short conversation you can play, and the choices are at most three lines.

**When not:** A literary conversation would be taken as the historically correct answer — use `myth-trap` instead.

**Steps:** Two or three choices each turn. The right choice teaches the idea. A wrong choice teaches "this is a story." Do not build a long branched RPG tree.

---

## 中文

**对话选择.** 问题：观念只在论文里，玩家没有「在场」感。

**何时用:** 有一场可演的短对话，选项不超过三句。

**何时不用:** 会把文艺对话当成史实正确答案——那时改用 `myth-trap`。

**步骤:** 每轮 2–3 选项。正确项教观念；错误项教「这是故事」。不要 branched RPG 长树。
