## game.balance-scale

## English

**Balance the scale.** Problem: Amounts are felt as "a lot" or "a little," and picking a sentence does not show the balance.

**When:** Two sides, and a handful of pieces whose weights or counts are in the brief. The goal is a named balance (level, or one side heavier).
**When not:** There are no amounts. Do not invent numbers so the page can have a toy.

**Steps:**

1. Show a scale. Pieces are buttons. Tap a piece, then tap the left pan or the right pan.
2. The scale tips in a way that matches the numbers. Also say the state in words: level, left heavier, right heavier.
3. At most six pieces. Tap to remove a piece back to the table.
4. `mark` when the scale matches the spec. No exact-pixel drag.

**Don't:** A physics playground with no target. Do not hide the goal.

**Example:** Three weights and a target of "level." Weights and the goal are in JSON. One section on the page.

## 中文

**把秤调平.** 问题：多少只被记成「很多」或「很少」。选一句话看不出平衡。

**何时用:** 两边，几块有重量或数量的东西，目标是简报写明的平衡（持平，或某一边更重）。
**何时不用:** 没有数量。不要为了有个玩具去编数字。

**步骤:**

1. 出示一架秤。物件是按钮。先点物件，再点左盘或右盘。
2. 秤的倾斜要和数字一致。同时用文字说状态：持平、左更重、右更重。
3. 最多六件。再点一次可以把物件放回桌上。
4. 秤符合规格才 `mark`。不要靠精确拖拽。

**反例:** 没有目标的物理沙盒。不要把目标藏起来。

**实例:** 三块砝码，目标是「持平」。重量和目标在 JSON 里。页面上一节。
