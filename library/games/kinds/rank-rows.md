## game.rank-rows

## English

**Rank into rows.** Problem: The order is a judgment (more to less), not a timeline, and a sorted list hides the choice.

**When:** One list, at most six items, and the brief names the row each item belongs in.
**When not:** The order is chronological (`game.sequence-order`), or nobody has agreed on a ranking.

**Steps:**

1. Two or three named rows. Tap an item, then tap a row.
2. A wrong row says, in words, which way it should move.
3. `mark` when every item is in the row the spec names.

**Don't:** A public board of the player's score. Rows that do not change the outcome.

**Example:** Rows "more citable / less citable" on a methods page. The row names come from the brief.

## 中文

**分排.** 问题：顺序是一种判断（多到少），不是时间线，排好的列表把选择藏起来了。

**何时用:** 一份名单，最多六项，简报写明每一项落在哪一排。
**何时不用:** 顺序是年代（用 `game.sequence-order`），或根本没有约定的高低。

**步骤:**

1. 两到三排，每排有名字。点一项，再点一排。
2. 放错就用文字说它该往哪边移。
3. 每一项都在规格那一排才 `mark`。

**反例:** 把玩家分数做成公开榜。排本身不影响结果。

**实例:** 方法页上的「更可引用 / 更不可引用」。排的名字来自简报。
