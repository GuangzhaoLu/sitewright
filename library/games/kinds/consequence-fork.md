## game.consequence-fork

## English

**Short fork.** Problem: The result of a judgment is only summarized at the end of the text. The player never walked it.

**When:** At most two levels, at most three branches each, and one branch is the misunderstanding named in the brief.

**When not:** A long dialogue tree. If a literary branch would be read as the historically correct answer, use `myth-trap`, or fold it into one scene of `dialogue-choice`.

**Steps:**

1. The first level taps a judgment. The second level shows only that judgment's direct result, then closes.
2. The misunderstanding branch: say this is a consequence. Do not write progress. The accurate branch: one sentence on why it stops here.
3. Do not write it as a long piece of fiction.
4. `mark` only when the player reaches the branch named in the spec.

**Don't:** Six levels of branches, each with a reward.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Unlike `dialogue-choice`: dialogue is one conversation, and this card is two steps of consequence after a judgment.

## 中文

**短分叉.** 问题：一个判断的后果只在文末被总结，玩家没走过。

**何时用:** 最多两层，每层 ≤3 支，其中一支是简报认定的误解。

**何时不用:** 长对话树。文艺分支会被读成史实正确答案——改 `myth-trap`，或收束成 `dialogue-choice` 的一场。

**步骤:**

1. 第一层点判断，第二层只展示该判断的直接后果，然后收束。
2. 误解支：说明这是后果，不写进度。准确支：一句为什么停在这里。
3. 不要写成架空长篇。
4. 走到规格那一支才 `mark`。

**反例:** 六层分支，每支都有奖励。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。与 `dialogue-choice` 不同：对话是一场面谈，这张是判断之后的两步后果。
