## game.spatial-board

## English

**Spatial board.** Problem: The relation is spatial (side by side, at the edge, a side branch), not a list.

**When:** A board variant of `parallel-place`, when you need to see who sits beside whom.

**When not:** Plain question and answer.

**Steps:** Slots have names. The keyboard can move focus onto a slot. On a narrow screen the slots are large enough.

---

## 中文

**空间棋盘.** 问题：关系是空间的（并排、边缘、旁支），不是列表。

**何时用:** `parallel-place` 的棋盘变体、需要看见「谁和谁并排」。

**何时不用:** 纯问答。

**步骤:** 槽有名字。键盘也可把焦点移到槽。窄屏槽要够大。
