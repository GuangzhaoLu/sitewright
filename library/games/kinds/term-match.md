## game.term-match

## English

**Term versus sense.** Problem: The everyday sense swallows the term.

**When:** You need to pull apart "I have heard this word, but I picture something else."

**When not:** The term has no common wrong sense.

**Steps:**

1. The term on the left, senses on the right. Tap left, then right.
2. **The right side must include a tappable wrong sense** (the everyday one people have heard). A mismatch shakes and shows a fixed wrong-case line.
3. `mark` only after every correct pairing is done.
4. The exit goes to the glossary.

**Don't:** The right side has only the correct sense. The wrong sense is written in the explanation and cannot be tapped.

**Example:** The wrong sense must be tappable. Written only in the explanation, it is not a round.

## 中文

**术语对坐.** 问题：日常义吞掉术语。

**何时用:** 要拆「听过这个词但想成另一回事」。

**何时不用:** 术语没有常见错义。

**步骤:**

1. 左侧术语，右侧义项。点左再点右。
2. **右侧必须有可点的错义**（听过的那个日常义）。配错摇头 + 固定错案文。
3. 配完所有正解才 `mark`。
4. 出口到术语表。

**反例:** 右侧只有正解，错义只写在说明里点不到。

**实例:** 错义必须能点到。只写在说明里不算一局。
