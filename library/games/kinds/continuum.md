## game.continuum

## English

**Degree scale.** Problem: A judgment that has degrees is flattened into right or wrong.

**When:** One named scale, three to five marks, and the brief states where each object lands.

**When not:** Only two poles; or discrete layers already use `sort-trays`. No landing you can check, only an attitude.

**Steps:**

1. Marks use words (closer to a record / either way / closer to a story), not color alone.
2. Tap the object, then tap the mark. Do not rely on dragging a slider.
3. One mark off: say why it is not further left or further right. On the mark: one sentence on why it stops here.
4. `mark` only when every object sits on the mark in the spec.

**Don't:** A slider that passes only at an exact percentage.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Unlike `you-draw-it`: that card checks a curve or a proportion, and this card checks a position on a scale of degree.

## 中文

**程度尺.** 问题：本来有程度的判断被压成对或错。

**何时用:** 一条有名字的尺，3–5 个刻度，对象有简报写明的落点。

**何时不用:** 只有两极；或离散分层已经用 `sort-trays`。没有可核对的落点，只有态度。

**步骤:**

1. 刻度用文字（更近记录 / 两可 / 更近故事），不只用颜色。
2. 点物件再点刻度。不要只靠拖滑块。
3. 放偏一格：说明为何不是更左或更右。放对：一句为什么停在这里。
4. 全部落在规格刻度才 `mark`。

**反例:** 滑块要精确到百分比才过关。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。与 `you-draw-it` 不同：那张对的是曲线或比例，这张对的是程度上的位置。
