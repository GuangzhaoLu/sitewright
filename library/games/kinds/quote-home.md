## game.quote-home

## English

**Send the quote home.** Problem: A quotation has left its passage, and the reader does not know where it lives.

**When:** The quotation returns to at most six chapters or nodes that already exist and can be linked.

**When not:** Those chapters do not exist yet. If it is only a word and a sense, use `term-match`.

**Steps:**

1. One excerpt, several homes (chapter buttons). Tap the excerpt, then tap the home.
2. A wrong home: say which layer it actually appears in (text, note, a later hand).
3. A right home: give the link to that section. An empty "being written" is not a home.
4. `mark` only when every excerpt is home.

**Don't:** The home's name was invented for this round and does not exist on the site.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Use it with `web.exit-to-lesson`: the exit after sending it home must be a real page.

## 中文

**引文归位.** 问题：摘句脱离篇章，读者不知道它住在哪。

**何时用:** 引文要回到 ≤6 个已经存在、链得开的章节或节点。

**何时不用:** 那些章节还不存在。只是词和义——用 `term-match`。

**步骤:**

1. 一句摘录，几个家（章节按钮）。点摘录再点家。
2. 归错：说它实际出现在哪一层（正文、注、后人）。
3. 归对：给出到该节的链接。空「撰写中」不算家。
4. 全部归对才 `mark`。

**反例:** 家的名字是这轮才临时造的，站上没有。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。和 `web.exit-to-lesson` 一起用：归位的出口必须是真页面。
