## game.sequence-order

## English

**Order in time.** Problem: Steps or years are remembered as a pile. Nobody can say what comes first.

**When:** The order itself is the thing to learn, there are at most six blocks, and the order is not a ladder of bosses.

**When not:** The reader would read earlier-and-later as causal evolution — use `timeline-branch` or `parallel-place`. If the brief bans a how-to you can practice, do not make it a process tutorial.

**Steps:**

1. Name slots 1…n. The note says "earlier / later," not "the next stage."
2. Tap a card, then tap an empty slot. A miss: say what it should come before.
3. Drag may assist. Tap must be enough to pass.
4. `mark` only when all are right.

**Don't:** An experience bar, or a map that unlocks the next chapter.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Unlike `reconstruction`: that card teaches blocks of a passage, and this card teaches order in time or in steps.

## 中文

**先后排序.** 问题：步骤或年代记成一堆，分不清谁先谁后。

**何时用:** 顺序本身是知识点，块 ≤6，且顺序不是升级打怪。

**何时不用:** 读者会把先后理解成因果进化——改 `timeline-branch` 或 `parallel-place`。简报禁止可跟练的操作步骤时，不要做成流程教程。

**步骤:**

1. 槽按 1…n 命名，旁注写「先 / 后」，不写「下一阶段」。
2. 点卡片再点空槽。放错：说清它应在谁之前。
3. 拖拽可做辅，点选必须能过关。
4. 全对才 `mark`。

**反例:** 经验条、解锁下一章的地图。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。与 `reconstruction` 不同：那张教篇章块，这张教时间或步骤先后。
