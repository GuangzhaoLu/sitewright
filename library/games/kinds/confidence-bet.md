## game.confidence-bet

## English

**Confidence first.** Problem: A right answer does not show whether it was a guess or real knowledge.

**When:** The question already has a right and a wrong, and you also want to teach calibration.

**When not:** There is no answer you can check. Do not use it for a leaderboard or to shame low confidence.

**Steps:**

1. Tap one of three confidence levels first (unsure / fairly sure / very sure), then answer. Both steps are buttons.
2. Feedback says both whether the answer is right and whether the confidence fits. A lucky hit says "this was a lucky hit."
3. Do not rank scores. Do not deduct learning progress for low confidence.
4. `mark` looks only at whether the answer matches the spec, not at the bet.

**Don't:** High confidence and a wrong answer wipe rounds that were already passed.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Classroom confidence weighting. It is the multiple-choice version of `you-draw-it`.

## 中文

**先押信心.** 问题：答对了也不知道是蒙的还是真会。

**何时用:** 题已有对错，还要教校准。

**何时不用:** 没有可核对答案。用来做排行榜或羞辱低信心。

**步骤:**

1. 先点信心三档（不稳 / 还行 / 很稳），再答题。两步都是按钮。
2. 反馈同时说：答案对不对、信心是否配得上。蒙对要说「这是蒙对」。
3. 不记分排名，不因低信心扣学习进度。
4. `mark` 只看答案是否按规格答对，不看押注。

**反例:** 信心高却答错就清掉已过的局。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。课堂信心加权；是 `you-draw-it` 的选择题版本。
