## game.memory-pairs

## English

**Memory pairs.** Problem: Two things belong together, and a table never makes the player commit.

**When:** Four to eight pairs, and the match itself is the lesson (term and sense, picture and name, claim and evidence).
**When not:** More than eight pairs, or the pictures are the only clue and they are tiny on a phone.

**Steps:**

1. Face-down cards in a grid. Tap one, then another. Both are buttons with readable labels once open.
2. A match stays open, with one sentence on why they belong. A miss turns them back.
3. Tap only. Drag is not required.
4. `mark` when every pair is open.

**Don't:** A chess clock. Color as the only thing that matches.

**Example:** Eight cards, four pairs, labels in JSON, one section under an article.

## 中文

**翻牌配对.** 问题：两样东西本该在一起，表格不会逼读者下手。

**何时用:** 四到八对，配上本身就是要学的（术语和释义、图和名字、主张和证据）。
**何时不用:** 超过八对，或只靠很小的图来认，手机上看不清。

**步骤:**

1. 背面朝上的格子。点一张，再点一张。翻开后都是能读的按钮。
2. 配上就留着，并给一句为什么它们是一对。配错就翻回去。
3. 只靠点按。不必拖。
4. 每一对都翻开才 `mark`。

**反例:** 棋钟。只用颜色当配对线索。

**实例:** 八张牌、四对，标签在 JSON 里，放在文章下面的一节。
