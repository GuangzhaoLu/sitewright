## game.choice-round

## English

**Multiple choice.** Problem: A paragraph is not enough. The page needs one question with an answer you can check.

**When:** One idea, three or four options, and the brief names the correct one.
**When not:** The job is sorting, pairing, or a popular line that must not win (`game.myth-trap`).

**Steps:**

1. Stem, then options as buttons. One of them is correct.
2. On tap, say in words why this option holds or fails. Lock the question before the next one.
3. Wrong options are plausible. One of them may be the common slip. They are not jokes.
4. `mark` only on the correct option.

**Don't:** Ten options, or a score that shames. Do not hide the criterion until after the win.

**Example:** One section, "check this idea," with the stem and options in JSON.

## 中文

**单选一题.** 问题：只给一段文章不够。这一页需要一道能核对的题。

**何时用:** 一个观念，三到四个选项，简报写明哪一个对。
**何时不用:** 要分类、配对，或有一句流行话不得当赢（用 `game.myth-trap`）。

**步骤:**

1. 题干，然后选项是按钮。其中有一个对。
2. 点下去就用文字说明这一项为什么成立或不成立。本题锁住再给下一题。
3. 错项要像那么回事。其中一个可以是常见的滑法。不要写成笑话。
4. 只有点中正确项才 `mark`。

**反例:** 十个选项，或用分数羞辱。不要把标准藏到通关之后。

**实例:** 页面上一节「核对这一个观念」，题干和选项放在 JSON 里。
