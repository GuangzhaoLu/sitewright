## game.claim-evidence

## English

**Hang evidence on a claim.** Problem: Once the conclusion and the source are pulled apart, they no longer match.

**When:** Two to four claims, each with one short piece of evidence, and the brief says the pairing is one to one.

**When not:** It is only a term and a sense — use `term-match`. If one piece of evidence may hang on more than one claim, and this round teaches that, do not make a single correct pairing.

**Steps:**

1. Claims on the left, evidence on the right. Tap left, then right.
2. Put material on the right that cannot be hung (a handsome sentence, a secondhand retelling).
3. A wrong hang: say in plain words which kind of source is missing.
4. `mark` only when every hang is right. The exit goes to an anchor in the source.

**Don't:** The evidence is longer than the claim, so the player passes by memorizing the pair.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. A classroom claim-and-evidence card.

## 中文

**主张挂证据.** 问题：结论和出处拆开之后对不上号。

**何时用:** 2–4 条主张，每条有一条短证据，且简报规定一对一。

**何时不用:** 只是术语和义项——用 `term-match`。一证多用且这轮要教「可以兼挂」时，不要做成唯一正解配对。

**步骤:**

1. 左主张，右证据。点左再点右。
2. 右侧放入不能挂上的材料（好看的句子、二手转述）。
3. 挂错：白话说明缺的是哪一种出处。
4. 全部挂对才 `mark`。出口到原文锚点。

**反例:** 证据写得比主张还长，玩家靠背诵配对。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。课堂 claim-and-evidence 卡片。
