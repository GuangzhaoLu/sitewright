## game.odd-one-out

## English

**Which one does not belong.** Problem: The edge of a category is fuzzy, and the exception is treated as one of the group.

**When:** A set of four to six items, and exactly one of them does not belong by a criterion you have written down.

**When not:** More than one item does not belong, or the criterion takes half a page to explain.

**Steps:**

1. The prompt states the criterion first ("which one cannot be a note"). Do not only say "spot the difference."
2. Each item is a button. Hitting the exception: a short reason it is outside. Hitting one of the group: point at the one thing they share.
3. Accept only one item. Do not make it a timed spot-the-difference.
4. A pass may `mark`. If it is only a warmup, the brief says it does not `mark`.

**Don't:** All four items are odd, and the player picks "the least like" by taste.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. A classroom which-doesn't-belong. In news literacy, "which one is not evidence."

## 中文

**哪一张不属.** 问题：类别边界含糊，例外被当成同类。

**何时用:** 一组 4–6 项里恰好一项按写明的标准不属于。

**何时不用:** 多项都不属，或标准要半页才能讲清。

**步骤:**

1. 题干先写标准（「哪一张不能当注」），不要只写「找不同」。
2. 每项是按钮。点中例外：短解释它为何出界。点中同类：指出它们共享的那一条。
3. 只收一项。不要做成限时找茬。
4. 过关可 `mark`；若只是热身，简报要写明不 `mark`。

**反例:** 四项都怪，靠审美选「最不像的」。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。课堂 which-doesn't-belong；新闻素养里「哪一条不是证据」。
