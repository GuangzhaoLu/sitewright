## game.reveal-cover

## English

**Reveal.** Problem: The answer is more convincing if the reader commits before seeing it.

**When:** One image, number, or sentence is covered, and the reveal is the payoff of a guess.
**When not:** There is nothing to guess. A decorative before-and-after with no question.

**Steps:**

1. Ask in text first. Then a cover over the answer.
2. The player taps a guess (two or three buttons). Then the cover clears.
3. The reveal includes one sentence on what it shows. The words still hold the answer if the picture is missing.
4. `mark` only if the guess matches the spec. Opening the cover without a guess does not `mark`.

**Don't:** A thin slider as the only control. Offer a Reveal button that is easy to hit.

**Example:** A chart stays covered until the player picks up, flat, or down. Then the line is shown. One tap, not a drawing.

## 中文

**先猜再揭.** 问题：读者先押一次，答案才站得住。

**何时用:** 一张图、一个数或一句话被盖住，揭开是猜测的回报。
**何时不用:** 没什么可猜的。没有问题的前后对比，只是装饰。

**步骤:**

1. 先用文字提问。答案上有一层遮盖。
2. 玩家点一个猜测（两个或三个按钮）。然后遮盖揭开。
3. 揭开时给一句它说明了什么。图没有时，文字仍然带着答案。
4. 只有猜测符合规格才 `mark`。不猜就揭开，不算过。

**反例:** 只能靠一条很细的滑杆。要有一块好点的「揭开」按钮。

**实例:** 图先盖住，玩家点上升、持平或下降，再出示曲线。一次点按，不是画画。
