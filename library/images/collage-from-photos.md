## img.collage-from-photos

## English

**Photo collage.** Problem: When the generated look will not stay consistent, use objects you photographed.

**Steps:** Keep the light coming from one direction. Pull the color toward the site's main colors. Do not collage it into an advertisement. Mind portrait rights.

---

## 中文

**实拍拼贴.** 问题：生成风不统一时，用自己拍的器物。

**步骤:** 统一光源方向；色彩靠向站内主色；不要拼成海报广告。注意肖像权。
