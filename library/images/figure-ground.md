## img.figure-ground

## English

**Separate figure and ground.** Problem: In a thumbnail the subject melts into the background, and only texture remains.

**When:** The subject still has to be recognizable at a small size.

**When not:** A style frame that deliberately lets the subject melt into a distant view — that is `img.paper-atmosphere`.

**Steps:**

1. The subject is one step lighter or darker than the ground. Use a difference in value, not only in hue.
2. Lower the contrast of background texture so it does not steal the outline.
3. Look again at card size.

**Don't:** The whole image is one gray, and the subject is a guess.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Check it together with `img.silhouette-read`.

---

## 中文

**图地分开.** 问题：缩略图里主体融进背景，只剩纹理。

**何时用:** 小尺寸仍要认出主体。

**何时不用:** 风格帧故意让主体融进背景的远景——那是 `img.paper-atmosphere`。

**步骤:**

1. 主体比背景深或浅一档，用明度差，不只用色相。
2. 背景纹理降低对比，不抢轮廓。
3. 缩到卡尺寸再看一次。

**反例:** 全图同一灰度，主体靠猜测。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。和 `img.silhouette-read` 一起检查。
