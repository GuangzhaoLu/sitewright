## img.paper-atmosphere

## English

**Distant atmosphere.** Problem: The cover is made as a close-up or as neon.

**When:** A cover, a hub card, or a chapter head, and the style frame is a distant view.

**When not:** You need a diagram that can be read (use SVG or real type in the layout). If the site's style frame is not this material, do not force a paper texture.

**Steps:** 16:9. A middle or far view. Leave space. Use the material and colors from the style frame. The accent is a touch. No neon glow.

**Don't:** A face close-up, oversized anime eyes, a cyber outline.

**Example:** The material follows the site that already exists. Sitewright does not prescribe one kind of paper or one kind of landscape.

## 中文

**远景氛围.** 问题：封面被做成特写或霓虹。

**何时用:** 封面、枢纽卡、章头，且风格帧定的是远景。

**何时不用:** 需要可读的图解（改 SVG 或真字体排版）。站的风格帧不是这一种材质时，不要硬套纸纹。

**步骤:** 16:9；中远景；留白；用风格帧里的材质和色，强调色点缀即可。无霓虹辉光。

**反例:** 脸部特写、二次元大眼、赛博描边。

**实例:** 材质跟已有站，不在 Sitewright 里规定某一种纸或某一种山水。
