## img.callout-lines

## English

**Callouts stay in the layout.** Problem: Arrows and numbers are painted into the landscape, so a copy change means redrawing.

**When:** A diagram needs to label parts.

**When not:** An atmosphere cover with no labels.

**Steps:**

1. The base image has no words and no arrows. Numbers and leader lines are SVG or HTML on top.
2. A leader line does not cross the subject's only outline.
3. On a narrow screen, switch to a numbered list under the image. The lines may collapse.

**Don't:** Numbers and garbled arrows burned into a raster.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. Use it with `img.type-stays-html`.

## 中文

**引线留在排版层.** 问题：箭头和编号画进风景，一改文案就重画。

**何时用:** 图解要标注部件。

**何时不用:** 无标注的氛围封面。

**步骤:**

1. 底图无字无箭头。编号和引线用 SVG 或 HTML 叠在上面。
2. 引线不要穿过主体的唯一轮廓。
3. 窄屏改为图下编号列表，引线可以收起。

**反例:** 栅格图里烧进编号和乱码箭头。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。配合 `img.type-stays-html`。
