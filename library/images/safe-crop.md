## img.safe-crop

## English

**Safe crop.** Problem: The same image goes into a 16:9 cover and a square card, and the subject is cut off.

**When:** One image has to serve more than one frame.

**When not:** It serves one fixed frame only.

**Steps:**

1. Put the subject in about the central sixty percent. Leave the edges for cropping.
2. Before export, crop once at 16:9 and once at 1:1. The subject survives both.
3. Do not park an object or a building on the edge. If the brief bans faces, a person is not the subject parked on the edge either.

**Don't:** A wide composition with the only subject at the far right, so a square crop leaves only paper texture.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. One file per frame. Do not let CSS crop away the only subject. Write the filename together with `img.cover-slot`.

## 中文

**安全裁切.** 问题：同一张图进 16:9 封面和方卡，主体被切掉。

**何时用:** 一张图要服务多于一个画幅。

**何时不用:** 只服务一个固定框。

**步骤:**

1. 主体放在画面中央约六成里，四边留给裁切。
2. 导出前用 16:9 和 1:1 各裁一次，主体都在。
3. 器物和建筑不要贴边。简报禁止脸时，人也不要作为主体贴边。

**反例:** 横构图把唯一主体放在最右侧，方裁后只剩纸纹。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。每个画幅一个文件，不靠 CSS 硬裁掉唯一主体。和 `img.cover-slot` 的文件名一起写死。
