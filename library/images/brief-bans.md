## img.brief-bans

## English

**Bans live in the brief.** Problem: A sensitive mark is drawn as something you can operate, or a subject the brief did not allow is drawn.

**When:** The brief lists images that must not appear.

**When not:** The brief has no image bans, and a portrait has permission.

**Steps:** Draw only what the brief allows (objects, buildings, landscape, interface, depending on the project). After generation, look again: readable fake text, and anything banned. Bans are not written into Sitewright cards.

**Don't:** Draw a mark the brief forbids clearly enough to copy.

---

## 中文

**禁画写在简报.** 问题：敏感符号被画成可操作物，或画成简报没有允许的主体。

**何时用:** 简报列出了不能出现的图像。

**何时不用:** 简报没有禁画，且肖像有授权。

**步骤:** 只画简报允许的东西（器物、建筑、风景、界面，视项目而定）。生成后再看：有没有可读假字，有没有禁画。禁画不写进 Sitewright 卡片。

**反例:** 把简报禁止的标记画清楚到能临摹。
