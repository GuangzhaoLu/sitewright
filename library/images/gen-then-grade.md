## img.gen-then-grade

**Generate, then grade.** Problem: Images arrive fast, and the rejects enter the site too.

**Steps:** Each image passes four questions: does the temperament match the site, is there fake text, is there anything banned, and is it mush after compression. A fail is generated again. ## 中文

**生成后评级.** 问题：出图很快，废图也进站。

**步骤:** 每张过四问——气质是否同站、有无假字、有无违禁物、压缩后是否糊。不及格重抽，不修涂成四不像。导出 JPG 质量 0.82–0.9，宽边 1600–2400。

**反例:** 把 AI 水印或英文乱字留在角落。
