## img.cover-slot

## English

**Reserve the slot, then fill it.** Problem: The frontend is left with an empty `src` or a leftover SVG placeholder.

**Steps:** The IA writes the filename, the frontend hangs the path first, and the art seat overwrites that name. "Image later" is not allowed.

---

## 中文

**先留槽再填图.** 问题：前端空 `src` 或 SVG 占位残留。

**步骤:** IA 写文件名 → 前端先挂路径 → 插图席位按名覆盖。禁止「图稍后」。
