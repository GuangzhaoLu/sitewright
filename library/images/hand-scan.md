## img.hand-scan

## English

**Draw, then scan.** Problem: Generated images will not hold a temperament, and the small site needs the feel of paper.

**When:** A diagram or an object drawing that a hand can finish.

**When not:** You need a photographic scene.

**Steps:**

1. Draw in pencil or ink on paper. The subject is large. Do not draw words onto the paper. Add words later.
2. Scan or shoot from above, straighten it, and pull the ground toward the site's main color.
3. Trace a diagram into SVG. Atmosphere may stay raster.
4. Export with `img.export-web`. The raw scan does not enter git.

**Don't:** A tilted phone photo, uncorrected trapezoid, used as a cover.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. The fallback when generation will not stay consistent.

## 中文

**手绘再扫描.** 问题：生成图气质不稳，小站又需要纸感。

**何时用:** 图解或器物稿，手能画完。

**何时不用:** 需要照片级的现场。

**步骤:**

1. 铅笔或墨线画在纸上，主体大。字不要画进纸，字后补。
2. 扫描或俯拍，校平，把底色收到站内主色附近。
3. 图解再描成 SVG；氛围可留栅格。
4. 按 `img.export-web` 导出。原扫描不进 git。

**反例:** 手机斜拍，梯形不校，当封面。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。生成不稳时的退路。
