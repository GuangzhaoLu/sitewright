## img.generative-log

## English

**Log the generation.** Problem: Next week nobody knows why this image was kept, or which one was rejected for fake text.

**When:** A generative model is making the images.

**When not:** A drawing or a photograph is already the final.

**Steps:**

1. Record: five lines of constraints, the filename you kept, and why others were rejected (fake text, a ban, temperament).
2. Do not make the full prompt the body of the document. Prompts expire. Constraints do not.
3. The log lives in the project's `out/`. It does not go on the public page.
4. Pass the four questions in `img.gen-then-grade` before it enters the repo.

**Don't:** Keep only a "final" and be unable to say what it replaced.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. When you remake an existing cover, keep the reason the old one was dropped.

## 中文

**生成记录.** 问题：下周没人知道这张为什么留下、哪张因假字淘汰。

**何时用:** 用生成模型出图。

**何时不用:** 手绘或实拍已定稿。

**步骤:**

1. 记：约束 5 行、采用的文件名、淘汰原因（假字、禁画、气质）。
2. 不把完整提示词当文档主体。提示词会过期，约束不会。
3. 记录放项目 `out/`，不放进线上页面。
4. 过 `img.gen-then-grade` 四问再入库。

**反例:** 只留一张「最终版」，说不清它替掉了什么。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。重出已有封面时，留下淘汰理由。
