## img.color-not-only

## English

**Color is not the only code.** Problem: Layers are only red and green. Some people cannot tell them apart, and they vanish in grayscale.

**When:** Color stands for a category, right or wrong, or a trap.

**When not:** A distant atmosphere shot with no category to read.

**Steps:**

1. A category also uses shape, position, or a text label.
2. A trap or a warning has a word as well as the accent color.
3. Look at the image in grayscale. The categories are still distinguishable.

**Don't:** A legend of three color swatches and no words.

**Example:** The same principle as `web.a11y-buttons`, applied to pictures. Sitewright games already require state in words.

## 中文

**色不是唯一编码.** 问题：分层只靠红绿，一部分人分不清，去色之后也没了。

**何时用:** 颜色在表示类别、对错、陷阱。

**何时不用:** 氛围远景没有类别要读。

**步骤:**

1. 类别同时用形状、位置或文字标签。
2. 陷阱和警告除了强调色，还要有词。
3. 把图去色看一遍，类别仍可分辨。

**反例:** 图例只有三个色块，没有字。

**实例:** 和 `web.a11y-buttons` 同一原则，用在图上。Sitewright 游戏里已要求状态用文字。
