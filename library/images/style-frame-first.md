## img.style-frame-first

## English

**Style frame first.** Problem: Each person (each agent) paints a different temperament.

**When:** You need new images, and the site already has images or a palette.

**When not:** You are only changing copy.

**Steps:**

1. Pull three representative images from the live site: ground color, empty space, whether there are people.
2. Write five lines of constraints (material, camera distance, forbidden things).
3. Make one style frame and review it, then produce the rest.
4. Filenames are written into the IA, so the hub does not break.

**Don't:** Generate twelve images and then pick the "handsome" ones.

---

## 中文

**先风格帧.** 问题：每人（每个 agent）画一种气质。

**何时用:** 要新图且站点已有图或色盘。

**何时不用:** 只改文案。

**步骤:**

1. 从现网抽 3 张代表图：底色、留白、有没有人物。
2. 写 5 行约束（材质、镜头距离、禁止物）。
3. 先出 1 张风格帧评审，再批量。
4. 文件名在 IA 里写死，避免枢纽裂图。

**反例:** 直接生成 12 张再挑「好看的」。
