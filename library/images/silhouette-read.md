## img.silhouette-read

## English

**Readable when small.** Problem: On a small card, only a blur remains.

**When:** The image will shrink to a hub card or a list.

**When not:** It is only a full-width distant view, and the silhouette was never the subject.

**Steps:**

1. Shrink the draft to about 64px tall and look at the silhouette. If you cannot name the object or the building, redraw it.
2. One subject. Do not let three things compete equally.
3. Delete hairline decoration. Leave that to SVG icons.

**Don't:** You know which chapter it is only by reading tiny words inside the image.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this yet. The thumbnail test used by editorial illustration.

## 中文

**缩了还能认.** 问题：小卡上只剩一片糊。

**何时用:** 图会缩到枢纽卡或列表。

**何时不用:** 只作全宽远景，剪影本来就不是主体。

**步骤:**

1. 把稿缩到约 64px 高看剪影。认不出器物或建筑轮廓就重画。
2. 主体一个，不要三件同样抢。
3. 细线装饰删掉，留给 SVG 图标。

**反例:** 靠读图里的小字才知道这是哪一章。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未用过。编辑插图的缩略图测试。
