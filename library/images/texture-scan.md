## img.texture-scan

## English

**Scan a texture.** Problem: A flat fill has no paper.

**When:** A background or an edge.

**When not:** It is the main image that tells the story.

**Steps:** Photograph paper, cloth, or a wall. Lower the contrast. Lay it over a color block. It does not outshout the illustration. Mind the license (you shot it, or the texture is clearly usable commercially).

---

## 中文

**纹理扫描.** 问题：纯平涂没有纸。

**何时用:** 背景、边缘。

**何时不用:** 当主视觉讲故事。

**步骤:** 拍纸、布或墙，压对比，叠在色块上，不抢插图。注意授权（自己拍或明确可商用纹理）。
