## img.svg-vs-raster

## English

**SVG for diagrams, raster for mood.** Problem: A diagram is painted as a landscape, and a landscape is built as a blurry SVG.

**When:** A catalog, layers, or a process.

**When not:** A cover that needs continuous texture.

**Steps:** Type inside SVG is real text (it can be copied), not fake letters. A raster does not carry words that must be read clearly.

---

## 中文

**图解用 SVG，氛围用栅格.** 问题：示意图画成风景，风景做成糊 SVG。

**何时用:** 目录、分层、流程。

**何时不用:** 需要连续纹理的封面。

**步骤:** SVG 里的字用真文字（可复制），不要画成假字。栅格图不要承担必须读清的字。
