## img.alt-text

## English

**Alt text.** Problem: A screen-reader user hears only "image," or hears a paragraph of mood.

**When:** The image carries information.

**When not:** Pure decoration, and the text beside it already says everything — `alt=""`.

**Steps:**

1. An infographic: state the relation that must be known. Do not write "the image shows."
2. An atmosphere image: one sentence of what is there. Do not write mood copy.
3. If the image has no words, the alt text does not invent a title on the picture.
4. The caption is for people who see it. Alt text does not repeat the whole caption.

**Don't:** Alt text that pastes the generation prompt.

**Example:** Sitewright has not used this card on its own yet. Any infographic that enters the site should get one.

## 中文

**替代文本.** 问题：读屏用户只听到「图」，或听到一段抒情。

**何时用:** 图承担信息。

**何时不用:** 纯装饰，且旁边文字已说完——`alt=""`。

**步骤:**

1. 信息图：写清必须知道的关系，不写「图片显示」。
2. 氛围图：一句景物，不写心情文案。
3. 图里若无字，alt 里也不要发明图上的标题。
4. 图注给看见的人；alt 不重复一整段图注。

**反例:** alt 贴生成提示词。

**实例:** Sitewright 尚未单独用过。任何进站的信息图都应补上。
