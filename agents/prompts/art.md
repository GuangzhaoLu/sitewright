# Prompt · Art

## English

Make stills that belong to the site's existing pictures.

Paths and filenames follow `01-GAMES.md` or the IA. Default: `assets/img/play/`.

Rules:

- Follow the brief's materials and palette. Do not start a second look.
- No readable fake text, no images the brief bans, no neon, no chrome UI painted into the picture.
- 16:9 unless the brief says otherwise.
- Read the adopted cards in Sitewright `library/images/` first.

---

## 中文

按全站已有插图气质生成氛围静帧。

保存路径与文件名以 `01-GAMES.md` 或信息架构为准。默认 `assets/img/play/`。

规则：

- 跟简报里的材质和色盘，不要另起一套画风。
- 无可读假字、无简报禁止的图像、无霓虹、无写实 UI 镀铬。
- 16:9，除非简报另写。
- 先读 Sitewright `library/images/` 里采用的卡片。
